# Paper map

Every table and figure in the paper, the stored results behind it, and how this release checks it.
Folders under `results/`, `records/`, `configs/` and `splits/` are in the dataset
[AgentsSci/ICLR2027-SafeToOmit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AgentsSci/ICLR2027-SafeToOmit)
(`make data`); the subset the checks and figures read is also committed here.

- **make figures**: the figure is regenerated byte for byte, and its script checks every plotted number
  against the paper's tables and the stored results.
- **make verify**: the number is re-derived from the per-question records.
- **stored results**: the experiment's stored outputs, with its protocol (`PREREG*.md` and its SHA-256),
  `SUMMARY.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md`, are in the dataset.

Experiment folders keep the experiment IDs used during the research (E3 … E22, X1 … X4).
